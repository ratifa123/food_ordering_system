## How to Order

### Step 1: Browse Our Menu
1. Open the Morogoro Taste Food app or website
2. Browse through our categories:
   - Burgers & Sandwiches
   - Pizza
   - Grilled Meats (Chicken, Beef, Fish)
   - Traditional Dishes (Ugali, Pilau, Biryani)
   - Beverages & Juices
   - Snacks & Sides

### Step 2: Select Food Items
1. Click on any food item to view details
2. Check the price, description, and availability
3. Click **"Add to Cart"** to add the item to your order

### Step 3: Adjust Your Cart
1. View your cart to see all selected items
2. Modify quantities if needed
3. Remove items you don't want
4. Review the total amount

### Step 4: Checkout Process

#### **If You Don't Have an Account:**
1. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**
2. Click **"Register/Login"** to create an account
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Password (create a secure password)
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. You will be redirected to the checkout page

#### **If You Already Have an Account:**
1. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**
3. Click **"Login"**
4. Your saved address and payment methods will appear
5. Proceed to payment

### Step 5: Enter Delivery Information
- **Delivery Address:** Enter where you want your food delivered
- **Phone Number:** Confirm your contact number
- **Delivery Instructions:** Add any special requests (e.g., "Leave at door", "Call upon arrival")
- **Preferred Delivery Time:** Select when you want your food

### Step 6: Choose Payment Method
Select one of our available payment options:
- **M-Pesa**
- **Tigo Pesa**
- **Airtel Money**
- **Pay on Delivery** (if available in your area)

### Step 7: Confirm Your Order
1. Review your complete order:
   - Food items and quantities
   - Subtotal
   - Delivery fee
   - Service fee
   - Tax
   - **Final Total Amount**
2. Click **"Place Order"**
3. Complete payment using your chosen method
4. You will receive an **Order Confirmation** via email and SMS

## Payment Methods

### M-Pesa
- Dial **\*150\*00# → Option 6 → Enter Merchant Code**
- Follow on-screen instructions to complete payment
- You will receive a confirmation message immediately

### Tigo Pesa
- Dial **\*150\*01# → Option 1 → Pay Bills**
- Enter Merchant Code and amount
- Confirm the transaction

### Airtel Money
- Dial **\*150\*00# → Enter PIN**
- Select payment option
- Enter merchant details and amount
- Confirm payment

### Pay on Delivery
- Pay in cash when your order arrives
- Available in selected areas only

## Delivery Information

### Delivery Areas
We deliver to all major areas within Morogoro, including:
- Mzumbe
- Ulanga
- Iringa Road
- City Center
- And surrounding neighborhoods

### Delivery Fees
- Standard Delivery: **TZS 2,500**
- Express Delivery: **TZS 3,500** (30 minutes or less)
- Free Delivery: On orders above **TZS 50,000**

### Estimated Delivery Time
- **30-45 minutes** for standard orders
- **20-30 minutes** for express delivery
- Times may vary based on order volume and traffic

### Delivery Status Tracking
1. After placing your order, you'll receive a confirmation
2. Track your order in real-time through the app
3. Receive SMS/app notifications for:
   - Order Confirmed
   - Order Being Prepared
   - Out for Delivery
   - Delivery Completed

## Order Status Explained

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Order received, awaiting confirmation |
| **Confirmed** | Order approved and being prepared |
| **In Progress** | Kitchen is preparing your order |
| **Out for Delivery** | Driver is on the way with your food |
| **Delivered** | Order successfully delivered |
| **Cancelled** | Order was cancelled by customer or admin |

## Support & Help

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Log into your account and click "Order Status" to see real-time tracking.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: You can only modify orders that are still "Pending". Contact us immediately if needed.

**Q: What if I don't receive my order?**
A: Contact our customer support within 1 hour of estimated delivery time.

**Q: How do I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are processed within 24-48 hours. Contact support with your order number.

**Q: Are there any discounts available?**
A: Yes! Check our promotions page for current offers and promo codes.
